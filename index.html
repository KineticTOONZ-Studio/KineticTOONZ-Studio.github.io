Who is KineticTOONZ?

Luke Hendrix (not his real name, but he prefers to also be called that), also known as KineticTOONZ, is an independent Singaporean multi-content creator born in 2009. He produces animations, digital artwork, and music.

His work often features a colorful cartoon style and original characters. As KineticTOONZ, he continues to expand his creative projects while sharing them with an online audience.

What he likes:

Animating, family, friends, YouTube friends, Dandy’s World, Friday Night Funkin’, and all of the good things.

What he dislikes:

People forcing him to do their requests, griefing in Roblox, people who think he’s weird for what he likes, and people stealing videos from other YouTubers and claiming them as their own.

Creeper and Slime are his two favourite monsters in Minecraft. Among his comfort media are the animated series Monster School and the song “Join Us For A Bite.”

History

History / YouTube Career

In 2021, when he was 12 years old, KineticTOONZ uploaded his first video on YouTube. At that time he did not have his own channel yet. The video was uploaded on his cousin’s channel called “Random Content” on October 19, 2021. The video was titled “Ada Hantu ke (Bahagian 1)”, which means “Is There a Ghost (Part 1)” in Malay.

A few months later, he received permission from his parents to create his own YouTube channel. His first channel, named “Kinetic Toons”, was created on December 5, 2021.

The Name

Before settling on the name Kinetic Toons, he considered other names such as Fastoons and Supertoons. He wanted the word “toons” in the name because of his love for cartoons.

One day in science class, he learned about kinetic energy. Since kinetic energy relates to the speed of an object, he decided to use it for his channel name.

The name “Kinetic Toons” represents both his fast pace in making videos and his love for cartoons.

Why He Moved to KineticTOONZ

Due to personal reasons, he was no longer able to upload on the Kinetic Toons channel. On November 7, 2022, he created KineticTOONZ as a fresh new start.

Content

He creates various types of videos including animations, skits, stories, and music.

He previously made Friday Night Funkin’ content, but over time he became less interested in it. On July 1, 2026, he left the FNF community to focus on making his own original content.

MUSIC ♪

He started making music back in 2021. At first he only had a keyboard. When he got FL Studio Mobile on his phone in December 2022, he began his music production journey properly.

Over time, he improved by learning music theory and studying many artists. Artists like Deco*27, Alan Walker and others inspired him, but Vocaloid inspired him the most.

His song “Forgetful Habit” was the first of his songs to get an Animated Music Video.

The song was originally named “Forgotten” in July 2024 and was supposed to be a Vocaloid song. He only used a free trial of Vocaloid 6 at the time, and the lyrics were completely different.
